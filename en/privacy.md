# Privacy policy

*Last updated: [TO COMPLETE: date]* · [Français](../../privacy/)

**Publisher.** Pépin is a mobile app published by [TO COMPLETE: publisher name, sole proprietor]. Contact: [TO COMPLETE: email].

**In short.** Pépin has no server, no user account, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your conversations stay on your phone.

## What Pépin keeps on your phone

- Your conversation with Pépin, your first name, the personality you picked, and the few things Pépin remembers about you ("What Pépin knows about me" screen).
- Your personal keys, if you add any, in the phone's secure vault (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).

All of this stays on your device. You can erase everything in Settings. Uninstalling the app also deletes it all.

## What leaves your phone, and only in these cases

1. **Downloading the local brain.** On first launch, the app downloads an AI model from Hugging Face. Hugging Face sees your phone's IP address and the file requested, as with any download. None of your data is sent. After that, Pépin thinks on your phone, with no internet needed.
2. **If you use your own keys.** Your messages (and the context needed: first name, summary, a few facts) are sent straight from your phone to the service you chose (Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI or Google's Gemini), with your key, with no middleman. Their privacy policy then applies and your account is used. Pépin asks for your consent before the first send.
3. **Voice dictation.** Speech recognition runs on your phone when possible. Otherwise your voice is transcribed by Apple's (iPhone) or Google's (Android) service, and Pépin tells you beforehand. Pépin does not record your voice.

## Permissions

Microphone (and speech recognition on iPhone): only for dictation, asked on first use. You can refuse.

## What Pépin does not do

Pépin sends nothing to its publisher, does not track you, shows no ads and shares none of your data.
